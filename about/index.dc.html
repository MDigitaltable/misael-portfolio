About Me

My Story

From Professional Kitchens to Digital Campaigns

I started my career in Baja California Sur, working as a second cook in a hotel kitchen after earning my bachelor's degree in gastronomy. I always dreamed of opening my own business, so I paid close attention to what made some restaurants succeed. I watched small places sell out from a single social media post, a lot like the story in the movie Chef.

I also saw the other side. Many great restaurants closed, not because of their food, but because they didn't know how to market their business. That's what pulled me into marketing. My love of cooking became a mission to help local restaurants and food brands build better strategies and keep improving, guided by the Kaizen philosophy of continuous improvement.

Today I'm a digital marketing student at Greystone College in Vancouver, building skills in Google Ads, social media marketing, SEO, content strategy and campaign measurement. The kitchen taught me to work under pressure, prep and plan ahead, stay consistent, work as a team and take feedback. I bring that same discipline to marketing: I plan campaigns around kitchen capacity, not just reach.
