Party Tray Paradise

Vancouver Party Tray Marketplace

The Challenge

Connect customers with affordable, multicultural party trays from local Vancouver restaurants.

Deliverables

Platform positioning, catalogue strategy and performance reporting.

My Approach

For this case study, I used an Excel dataset to create a marketing dashboard and analyze Party Tray Paradise’s website performance.

I reviewed users, sessions, engagement, conversions, revenue, channels, locations, and devices. Based on the results, I gave recommendations to improve traffic, conversions, local targeting, and customer experience.
